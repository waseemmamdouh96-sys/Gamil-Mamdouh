01 · About

Two disciplines, one engineering mindset

How a STEM education and years of hands-on training turned into practical embedded and web engineering skills.

I'm a STEM student at Assiut STEM School (expected graduation 2027, GPA 3.5 across Grades 10–11) who spends most of my time where hardware and software meet — wiring up sensors, reading datasheets, and then building the interface or dashboard that makes the data useful.

Over the past three years I completed five individual embedded-systems and software projects through the Digital Egypt Cubs Initiative — a nanodegree-style program sponsored by Egypt's Ministry of Communications and Information Technology (MCIT) and managed by Udacity — progressing through verified program levels up to Level Three: Embedded Systems and Robotics.

Alongside that, I contributed to four engineering capstone projects as part of Assiut STEM School teams, and independently designed, built, and deployed three freelance web systems end to end — twelve substantial technical projects in total, consistently grounded in research, iterative testing, and measurable results.

That combination is why I describe myself with two directions instead of one: Embedded Systems & Robotics for the physical, sensor-driven side of engineering, and Web Development for the interfaces, dashboards, and platforms — usually built on Firebase — that sit on top of it.

Embedded Systems

Microcontrollers, sensors, and firmware that read the physical world reliably.

Robotics & Automation

Turning sensor input into control logic — motors, actuators, and automated behavior.

IoT & Connectivity

Getting embedded devices onto the network so their data is actually usable.

Web Development

Frontend and Firebase-backed backend work that turns raw project data into something people can use.
